# Attention followups

AI-assisted exploratory experiments. Inspect the implementation and evidence before drawing conclusions.

These are controlled adaptations inspired by [Graphormer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.05234), [Swin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.14030) and [RoFormer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.09864). No third-party code was copied. Swin supplies locality inspiration only; there are no image windows over sensor-list order.

Spatial tokens are self plus the three strongest direct and three strongest strict two-hop neighbours. Selection uses S=max(A,A.T), zero diagonal, then S squared with self and direct entries removed. Stable sensor-index ties and zero-strength padding are fixed before fitting. Every variant receives the same 25 historical speed/observation/origin log-gap-age values, 16 sensor embedding values and three edge fields (A[neighbour,local], A[local,neighbour], self flag). Pair relations encode (forward bucket, reverse bucket) as 4*forward+reverse. Buckets are self=0, declared direct=1, declared two-hop=2, other=3. Direct takes precedence. These labels describe supplied matrix orientation, not verified traffic flow. Invalid keys are masked; padded outputs are discarded. The local output is invariant to matching permutations of nonlocal tokens and pair relations.

Spatial width is 16, two heads, FFN32, one pre-norm residual block, dropout .1. Uniform zeros QK scores and relation embeddings; content enables QK only; bias enables relation embeddings only; combined enables both. All trainable tensors and initialization are identical. Disabled branches receive zero gradients. Bias-only learns static relation-dependent weights. Edge content remains available to every variant. The unchanged local/spatial MLP backbone, persistence anchor and horizon calendar head are shared. Attention weights are not causal explanations.

Temporal tokens are the 12 available historical timestamps, using the existing seven local features (filled speed, observation flag and historical calendar), projected to width32. One pre-norm block has four heads and FFN64. Fixed sinusoidal positions are added to tokens for sinusoidal only. Rotary positions rotate adjacent pairs of Q/K coordinates at frequencies 10000^(-2i/8), before dot products scaled by sqrt(8). Both policies have identical trainable tensors. All historical tokens attend bidirectionally. Pooling uses the last historical token for both. The existing local history/gap-age encoder, sensor and target-calendar embeddings, one-hop spatial branch, persistence anchor and shared direct three-horizon residual MLP head remain identical. Known target calendar is allowed; future traffic and observation flags are not inputs.

Both studies use purged training and reused benchmark validation, train-only normalization/fallback, seeds42/43/44, one million fresh rows per epoch, batch2048, 50 epochs maximum, AdamW .001 and weight decay .0001, clip1, plateau halving patience3, early stop8. Selection minimizes macro validation MAE at steps3/6/12. Original test is excluded. Three optimization seeds on reused validation do not establish significance or time generalization. Comparisons to older MLPs have architecture and sampling confounds.

The spatial model has 97,033 trainable parameters per variant. The temporal model has 122,457 per variant. Training starts are 0 through 23,962; validation starts are 23,974 through 27,398. Training observed-speed mean and standard deviation are 58.584621 and 12.822317 mph. The last training target precedes the first validation target. Source, raw data, aligned arrays, sampling seeds, initial trainable hashes and optimizer policy are recorded in each frozen protocol.

A training-only 500-batch engineering pilot on a generic GPU workstation took 1.33 seconds for spatial and 1.10 seconds for temporal training, excluding feature setup. Peak allocated memory was below 1.4 GB. The frozen limits remain 900 seconds per fit and 10,800 seconds for the sequence. Full-fit epoch timings synchronize the GPU and include validation; feature setup is recorded separately. Diagnostic subgroups use observed target speed below 20 mph, and origins with an observed 60-minute target at least 10 mph below the observed origin speed. Diagnostics do not select checkpoints.

## Spatial results

Validation macro MAE, mean ± sample standard deviation across three seeds, is 2.91915 ± 0.00492 mph for uniform, 2.90970 ± 0.00257 for content, 2.91555 ± 0.00355 for bias-only, and 2.90923 ± 0.00649 for combined. Corresponding macro RMSE is 5.98026, 5.95569, 5.97452 and 5.96549 mph.

Paired macro MAE differences are content minus uniform −0.00945 ± 0.00260 mph (3/3 improved), combined minus content −0.00047 ± 0.00678 (2/3), bias-only minus uniform −0.00360 ± 0.00158 (3/3), and combined minus bias-only −0.00632 ± 0.00754 (2/3). Adding bias to content increases macro RMSE by 0.00981 mph on average. The results give little support for the extra structural bias in this setting. The tiny mean MAE gain is smaller than its variation across optimization seeds. No retuning follows this result.

The frozen protocols retain the replication runner's generic phrase “wider encoder than historical baseline” in the confounds field. For the spatial study, Transformer width remains 16, matching the earlier four-token attention model. Its neighbourhood changes from four to seven slots. Historical comparisons therefore mix neighbourhood and training differences; this study makes only its matched within-study claims. The temporal model also changes token definition and backbone capacity relative to earlier models. The frozen files are retained unchanged for provenance.

## Temporal results and limits

Sinusoidal macro MAE is 2.91759 ± 0.00339 mph; RoPE is 2.90773 ± 0.00295. Macro RMSE is 5.99507 ± 0.01592 and 5.98240 ± 0.00616 respectively. Paired RoPE minus sinusoidal macro MAE is −0.00986 ± 0.00581 mph, with improvements in all three seeds. Paired macro RMSE is −0.01267 ± 0.02181, with improvements in two seeds. Horizon-specific MAE differences at steps 3, 6 and 12 are −0.00862, −0.00898 and −0.01198 mph. This is a small validation gain for this encoding policy, not a general claim about temporal attention or GRUs.

Seventeen fits reached the 50-epoch cap. Seed-44 sinusoidal stopped after 35 epochs and selected epoch 27. The sequence totals 885 million sampled row exposures. They do not represent 885 million independent examples. Every overlapping epoch has matched sample hashes within each study and seed. Every selected checkpoint restores exactly to its saved prediction array, and recomputed validation metrics match. Source/data hashes and the before/after prior-output snapshot pass verification. The full suite passes 65 tests; the full repository lint check passes. Raw data, large predictions and checkpoints remain outside the commit. No original test results were computed.

For inspection, read the frozen protocol first, then per_seed.csv and paired_deltas.csv in each study namespace. aggregate.csv and paired_aggregate.csv report means and sample standard deviations. history.csv retains each epoch and sample hash; selection.json retains best epochs and checkpoint/prediction hashes; runtime.json separates setup time. diagnostics.csv contains subgroup results and exposure.csv records budget use. Training curves show checkpoint selection and whether the cap limits interpretation. These artifacts support review of the implementation and evidence.
